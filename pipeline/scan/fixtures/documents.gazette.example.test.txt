DECRETO LEGISLATIVO 5 agosto 2026, n. 118
Attuazione della direttiva (UE) 2023/970 del Parlamento europeo e del Consiglio, del 10 maggio 2023, volta a rafforzare l'applicazione del principio della parità di retribuzione tra uomini e donne.
(Courtesy translation of the operative provisions; the Italian text prevails.)

Art. 1 — Scope
1. This decree applies to all employers in the private and public sectors and to all workers who have an employment contract or employment relationship.

Art. 4 — Reporting on the gender pay gap
1. Employers with at least one hundred employees shall report, by 30 June of each year starting from 2027, the gender pay gap, the median gender pay gap, and the proportion of female and male workers in each quartile pay band.
2. The report shall be transmitted to the Ministry of Labour and Social Policies in the manner laid down by ministerial decree and published on the employer's website.
3. Employers with between one hundred and one hundred and forty-nine employees shall report every three years.

Art. 5 — Pay transparency prior to employment
1. Applicants for employment have the right to receive information about the initial pay or its range for the position concerned before the interview.
2. Employers shall not ask applicants about their pay history during their current or previous employment relationships.

Art. 6 — Joint pay assessment
1. Where the report reveals a difference in average pay level between female and male workers of at least five per cent in any category of workers, which the employer has not justified on the basis of objective, gender-neutral criteria, and has not remedied within six months, the employer shall carry out a joint pay assessment in cooperation with the workers' representatives.

Art. 9 — Penalties
1. Failure to submit the report is punished with an administrative fine of between 1,000 and 10,000 euro for each month of delay in the submission of the report.

Art. 12 — Entry into force
1. This decree enters into force on the fifteenth day following its publication in the Gazzetta Ufficiale.

DECRETO 21 agosto 2026
Modalità di trasmissione del rapporto sul divario retributivo di genere di cui all'articolo 4 del decreto legislativo 5 agosto 2026, n. 118.
(Courtesy translation.)

Art. 1
1. The report referred to in Article 4 of Legislative Decree No. 118 of 2026 shall be transmitted exclusively through the Servizi Lavoro portal of the Ministry of Labour and Social Policies.
2. Employers shall use the electronic form annexed to this decree; reports transmitted in any other format are deemed not submitted.

Art. 2
1. The first transmission window opens on 1 March 2027 and closes on 30 June 2027.
2. For subsequent years the window opens on 1 March and closes on 30 June.

Art. 3
1. The portal issues a receipt bearing the date and time of transmission, which constitutes proof of compliance with the reporting obligation.

ISPETTORATO NAZIONALE DEL LAVORO
Circolare n. 7 del 30 luglio 2026 — Prime indicazioni operative sugli obblighi di trasparenza retributiva.
(Courtesy translation.)

1. Headcount. The headcount threshold is calculated as the average number of employees in the calendar year preceding the report, counting part-time workers pro rata and fixed-term workers for the period actually worked.

2. Group companies. Each legal entity is an employer for the purposes of the threshold; the headcount of a corporate group is not aggregated.

3. Inspections. Inspection activity concerning the new pay transparency obligations will begin only after the first reporting deadline; until then inspectors will limit themselves to information and guidance.

AVVISO DI RETTIFICA
Comunicato relativo al decreto legislativo 5 agosto 2026, n. 118.
(Courtesy translation.)

In Article 4(3) of Legislative Decree No. 118 of 5 August 2026, the words "every three years" are corrected to "every three years, the first report being due in 2027". The remainder of the provision is unchanged.

Comunicato — Tasso di interesse legale per il secondo semestre 2026.
