How to use Press Sewa Portal 

FOR OWNER OF A PERIODICAL

  1. Sign up as owner by filling required details on this Portal.
  2. Log in using credentials created by you
  3. Select the periodicals owned by you from the list.
  4. Fill up your application by clicking the Registration Tab
  5. Add your publisher by providing contact details (email ID and mobile No.)
  6. Submit the online Registration Form

Further steps for registration will have to be taken by the publisher on the portal. The publishers may read the Advisory for registration of periodicals (PDF, 918 KB) . The owner should ask the printer to sign up on Press Sewa Portal separately.

FOR PRINTER (OWNER/KEEPER OF PRINTING PRESS)

  1. Sign up as Printer by filling the details
  2. Log in using credentials created by you
  3. Select your Printing Press in case it is available in the database; if not available already, add the details.
  4. Also add the periodicals printed by your press
  5. Update your profile

The printer should accept or reject the Publisher’s request to be appointed as Printer. The printer may read the Advisory for creation of Printing Press Profile (PDF, 801 KB) . The owner should ask the printer to sign up on Press Sewa Portal separately.
