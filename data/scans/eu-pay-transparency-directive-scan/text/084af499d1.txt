AVVISO DI RETTIFICA
Comunicato relativo al decreto legislativo 5 agosto 2026, n. 118.
(Courtesy translation.)

In Article 4(3) of Legislative Decree No. 118 of 5 August 2026, the words "every three years" are corrected to "every three years, the first report being due in 2027". The remainder of the provision is unchanged.
