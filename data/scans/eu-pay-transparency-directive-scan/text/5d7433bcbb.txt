Referentenentwurf des Bundesministeriums für Arbeit und Soziales
Entwurf eines Gesetzes zur Änderung des Entgelttransparenzgesetzes — Umsetzung der Richtlinie (EU) 2023/970
(Courtesy translation of the explanatory summary and key provisions.)

A. Problem and objective
The Directive must be transposed by 7 June 2026. The existing Entgelttransparenzgesetz covers the individual right to information only for establishments with more than 200 employees and reporting only for employers with more than 500 employees.

B. Solution
The draft extends the individual right to information to all employers regardless of size.
Employers with 100 or more employees shall report on the gender pay gap annually; employers with 250 or more employees shall report for the first time in respect of the 2026 calendar year.
Where the report shows an unjustified pay gap of at least 5 per cent, a joint pay assessment with the works council is mandatory.
The draft provides for fines of up to 100,000 euro for failure to report.

C. Procedure
The consultation period for the associations ends on 30 September 2026. Cabinet adoption is planned for the fourth quarter of 2026.
