Frequently asked questions — reporting duties for employers with 100 to 249 employees
(Courtesy translation.)

Q: When is the first report due?
A: Employers with between 100 and 249 employees will report every three years; the first report is due by 7 June 2031 under the Directive's timetable, but the draft bill proposes an earlier national deadline.

Q: What applies until the amending Act is adopted?
A: Until the amending Act enters into force, the existing Entgelttransparenzgesetz continues to apply and no new obligations arise.

Q: How are employees counted?
A: The headcount is determined at the reference date fixed in the amending Act; the draft uses 31 December of the reporting year.
